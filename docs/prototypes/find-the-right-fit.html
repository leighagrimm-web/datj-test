How a row is chosen: classes are grouped by style from their names (Tap Basics, Tap I, Tap I/II… are all Tap). For a new dancer, the row is that style’s lowest level that fits the age: Basics, Intro to, Tiny or a level I class. “Already dancing?” lists the levels above it that fit the age. Classes with no level in the name, like Bollywood or Conditioning, count as open to everyone.
